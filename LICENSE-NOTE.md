These materials are released under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International (CC BY 4.0)** — see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

**Author attribution is temporarily withheld.** Related academic work is
currently under double-blind peer review, and naming the authors here
would compromise that process. This is standard practice for anonymous
research artifacts.

Until attribution is added:

- You may use, adapt, and redistribute these materials freely under
  CC BY 4.0.
- Please attribute to this repository:
  `Text Flows Workshop — https://github.com/Text-Flows-Workshop/materials`
- Full author attribution will be added to this file and the README at
  the end of the review period, and the repository-URL attribution above
  will remain valid indefinitely.

Why CC BY rather than a share-alike or non-commercial license: the goal
of this release is for every AI-literacy effort — inside universities and
out — to be able to borrow from, adapt, and improve on these materials
with as little friction as possible.
