# Results — aggregate evidence from the first delivery (July 1, 2025)

Everything in this directory is **aggregate-only** by design: no
participant-level rows, names, or free-text responses are released.

## Attendance

- **61 participants** joined the session's live-interaction platform
  (join window spanning the ~3-hour session). This is the primary
  attendance record; attendees who never connected to the platform are
  not counted, so it is a floor.

## Live-polling feedback (n = 18 respondents, 4 questions)

The session's polling tool ran a four-question post-talk feedback poll
("What did you think of today's talk?", "What did you find most
interesting?", "Any comments about today?", "Any suggestions, or
applications you love?"). Eighteen participants responded. *(An earlier
version of this page said 19: the export's metadata row had been counted
as a respondent — corrected September 2026.)*

Ratings of "What did you think of today's talk?" (1–5 scale, all 18
respondents answered):

| Rating | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Count | 13 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 1 |

A mostly enthusiastic but genuinely mixed reception. Free-text
responses are not released; the question themes are reflected in the
de-identified [FAQ](../workshop/faq.md), which was built from the 15
questions submitted through the same platform.

## Honest limitations

- The formal survey's n = 5 is a small, self-selected fraction of the
  ~61 attendees; treat it as qualitative signal, not measurement.
- No pre/post skill-uptake instrument was administered at this
  delivery. Claims about the workshop's effect on tool adoption are
  therefore **not** made in these materials.
- The 15 attributed live-polling questions (de-identified in the FAQ)
  and the session transcript are the richest evidence of engagement;
  the transcript is not released because participant voices cannot be
  cleanly de-identified.
